About Monika Ravi

Ensuring Precision, Safety & Compliance in Human Clinical Trials

As a **Clinical Research Coordinator at Dhanvanthari Clinical Research Solutions**, Monika Ravi leads site operations for clinical trials with an unwavering focus on patient safety, protocol adherence, and regulatory excellence.

Her cross-functional approach Bridges trial sponsors, principal investigators, ethical boards, and research participants. By applying meticulous data management and robust Good Clinical Practice (GCP) guidelines, Monika accelerates the path from medical innovation to life-changing therapies.
