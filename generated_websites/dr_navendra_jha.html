About Dr. Navendra Jha

Empowering Medical Excellence Through Innovation & Mentorship

As an Assistant Professor at Viraat Ramayan Institute of Medical Sciences, Dr. Navendra Jha is committed to shaping the future of medicine through academic rigor, modern research methodologies, and clinical instruction.

With an unwavering focus on bridging theoretical medical science and compassionate clinical delivery, Dr. Jha fosters an engaging environment for medical students, researchers, and clinical trainees alike.
