Combining Strategic Insight with Unmatched Design Excellence

As a Senior Designer at Equinox Design OPC Pvt Ltd, I bridge the gap between creative philosophy and meticulous execution. My design approach centers on human experience, architectural harmony, and modern visual aesthetics to create products and spaces that captivate and inspire.

Whether defining brand systems, conceptualizing immersive user journeys, or leading multi-disciplinary creative directions, I bring strategic clarity, spatial intelligence, and precision to every undertaking.
