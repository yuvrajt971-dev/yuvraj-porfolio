About Dr. Harish Selvaraj

Pioneering High-Precision Microscopic Endodontics

Dr. Harish Selvaraj is a dedicated Micro Endodontist currently affiliated with Saveetha Dental College and Hospital. With a steadfast commitment to preserving natural dentition, Dr. Selvaraj specializes in high-magnification endodontic procedures that guarantee superior clinical outcomes.

By integrating advanced dental operating microscopes, 3D CBCT imaging, and modern nickel-titanium instrumentation, he delivers painless, efficient, and highly predictable endodontic care for even the most intricate root canal anatomy.

Clinical Services

Specialized Micro Endodontic Care

Combining surgical microscopes and modern techniques for painless, long-lasting outcomes.

Microscopic Root Canal Therapy

Execution of root canal treatments under dental operating microscopes, ensuring identification of extra canals and hidden anatomy.

Endodontic Retreatment

Specialized correction and retreatment of previously failed or reinfected root canal procedures to save compromise teeth.

Apicoectomy & Microsurgery

Precision surgical removal of root tip infections using micro-instruments, ultrasonic tips, and bioceramic retro-fillings.

Dental Trauma Management

Comprehensive endodontic intervention for fractured, avulsed, or luxated teeth to restore structure and vitality.

Regenerative Endodontics

Biologically-based procedures designed to replace damaged canal structures and promote continued root development in immature teeth.

3D CBCT Diagnostics

High-resolution Cone Beam Computed Tomography evaluation for thorough 3D assessment of complex root canal anatomy.
