About Dr. Rajeshwari

Dedicated to Excellence in Modern Dentistry & Patient Wellness

Dr. Rajeshwari Tambolkar is a distinguished Senior Dentist associated with the renowned Smile and Profile Dental Chain. With extensive clinical expertise, she specializes in aesthetic smile design, complex restorative treatments, and preventative dental wellness.

Her patient-centric approach blends compassionate care with state-of-the-art dental technology, ensuring every patient enjoys a calm, pain-free environment while achieving a healthy, radiant smile.
