About Dr. Pooja Kulkarni

Championing Patient-Centric Excellence & Modern Dentistry

As the Clinic Head at Clove Dental, Dr. Pooja Kulkarni orchestrates a seamless blend of clinical expertise, administrative governance, and cutting-edge dental technology. Her leadership ensures that every patient receives personalized, evidence-based treatments within an empathetic and modern clinical environment.
