Professional Profile

Empowering Future Dentists & Providing Exceptional Patient Care

Dr. Vijayalaxmi Kokatnur serves as an Assistant Professor at the prestigious P M Nadagowda Memorial Dental College & Hospital. Combining deep academic rigour with comprehensive clinical methodology, she is deeply committed to mentoring undergraduate and postgraduate dental scholars while providing superior oral health care.

Through evidence-based clinical protocols, interactive teaching strategies, and ongoing dental research, Dr. Kokatnur strives to advance the standards of contemporary dental medicine and encourage innovation in clinical procedures.
