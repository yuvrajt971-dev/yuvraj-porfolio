About Piyush Patel

Crafting High-Performance Interiors at Core Space Design

As a Senior Interior Designer at Core Space Design Consultants Pvt Ltd, Piyush Patel leads transformative interior architecture projects. With a deep passion for balance, texture, and light, Piyush translates client aspirations into immersive, highly functional physical environments.

From corporate headquarters to bespoke residential sanctuaries, his holistic approach oversees conceptual modeling, material specification, lighting dynamics, and flawless site implementation.
