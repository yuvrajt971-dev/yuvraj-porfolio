Dedicated to Providing Compassionate, World-Class Oral Healthcare

At Care 32 Dental Clinic, Dr. Aysha Thasneem Faizal combines clinical excellence with a gentle, patient-centric approach. Believing that every smile is unique, she utilizes advanced diagnostics and personalized treatment plans to ensure pain-free, long-lasting outcomes.
