About Dr. Sarita Bhandari

Bridging Rigorous Academic Theory with Modern Clinical Care

As an Associate Professor at Saveetha Dental College and Hospital, Dr. Sarita Bhandari is dedicated to cultivating the next generation of dental professionals while advancing evidence-based treatment practices.

Academic Leadership

Mentoring undergraduate and postgraduate dental students, conducting curriculum innovations, and supervising clinical rotations.

Clinical Research & Publications

Authoring impactful publications in indexed healthcare journals, focusing on modern oral pathology and restoration technology.

Specialization & Services

Comprehensive Academic & Clinical Offerings

Combining academic precision with compassionate patient care across multiple specialized domains.
