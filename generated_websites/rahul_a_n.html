About Rahul A. N

Bridging Technical Mastery & Spatial Vision at Parul University

As a dedicated Junior Architect at Parul University, Vadodara, Rahul combines analytical precision with modern aesthetic concepts to deliver spatial designs that inspire and endure.

Focusing on institutional spaces, commercial complexes, and contemporary residential layouts, he utilizes advanced BIM tools, parametric modeling, and eco-responsive building techniques to optimize both form and function.
