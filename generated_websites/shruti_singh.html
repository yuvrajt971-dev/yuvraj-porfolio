About Shruti Singh

Transforming Spaces into Soulful, Functional Works of Art

As a lead designer at Tranquil Decor, I specialize in blending modern minimalist architecture with warm, tactile luxury. My approach centers on listening to the unique lifestyle of clients, curating environments that radiate serenity, comfort, and architectural elegance.

From high-end residential estates to modern corporate headquarters, every project receives meticulous attention to light, spatial dynamic, custom furniture design, and sustainable material selection.
