About Dr. Tanishka Garg

Dedicated to Rebuilding Confidence, One Smile at a Time.

As a specialized Prosthodontist at Prosthodontist Dental Specialist, Dr. Tanishka Garg brings advanced expertise in diagnosing, planning, and executing complex restorative and cosmetic procedures.

Comprehensive Prosthodontic Care
