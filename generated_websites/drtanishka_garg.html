About Dr. Tanishka Garg

Dedicated to Artistry & Functional Restorations

Dr. Tanishka Garg is an accomplished Prosthodontist committed to delivering transformational oral rehabilitations and aesthetic smile designs. Operating at the intersection of modern prosthetics and biological preservation, Dr. Garg specializes in complex crown and bridge work, full-mouth reconstructions, dental implants, and CAD/CAM prosthetics.

With a compassionate approach tailored to individual anatomical needs, Dr. Garg ensures that every restoration delivers both seamless functional harmony and breathtaking natural aesthetics.
