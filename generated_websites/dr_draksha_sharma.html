About Dr. Draksha Sharma

Dedicated to Precision, Safety & Patient Comfort

Dr. Draksha Sharma is a highly skilled General Dentist serving at Arogyadham Dharmarth Hospital. With a patient-first ethos, Dr. Sharma combines clinical knowledge with advanced procedural standards to deliver quality oral healthcare accessible to all.

Specializing in routine examinations, preventive protocols, aesthetic restorative treatments, and patient education, Dr. Sharma ensures every visit is comfortable, transparent, and aligned with long-term dental health.
