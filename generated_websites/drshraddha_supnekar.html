Core Capabilities

Specialized Maxillofacial Radiology Services

Delivering thorough, high-resolution diagnostic interpretations tailored for precision clinical and surgical execution.

3D CBCT Volumetric Analysis

Detailed cross-sectional and 3D volumetric assessments of dentoalveolar structures, nerve mapping, and bone density evaluation.

Implant Diagnostic Planning

Sub-millimeter bone volume measurement, alveolar ridge mapping, and vital anatomical structure distance localization for surgical guides.

TMJ & Airway Assessment

Comprehensive condylar morphological imaging, TMJ disc space mapping, and volumetric airway analysis for sleep apnea & TMD patients.

Pathology Detection & Mapping

Radiographic differential diagnosis for jaw cysts, tumors, osteomyelitis, fibro-osseous lesions, and impacted teeth localization.

Endodontic Micro-Imaging

High-resolution localized CBCT reporting for complex root canal anatomy, missed canals, root fractures, and periapical pathology.

Second Opinion Reporting

Expert radiology consultation and second-opinion radiologic evaluations for complicated or ambiguous maxillofacial scans.
