About Dr. Bhole

Dedicated to Clinical Excellence & Community Welfare

As an Associate Dentist at Mother Teresa Memorial Charitable Dental Hospital and Research Centre, Dr. Nikita Bhole delivers comprehensive dental procedures with a focus on patient comfort, preventative care, and advanced oral health solutions.

Her practice balances high-standard therapeutic clinical interventions with accessible, community-driven care, ensuring that every patient receives compassionate, top-quality treatment designed for long-term oral wellbeing.

Patient-Centric Care

Gentle techniques tailored to individual comfort.

Modern Diagnostics

Precision diagnosis and evidence-based treatments.

Clinical Services

Comprehensive Dental Solutions

Delivering a full spectrum of preventative, restorative, and aesthetic oral healthcare services.

General & Preventive Dentistry

Routine dental checkups, comprehensive oral hygiene cleaning, flouride treatments, and early cavity detection.

Restorative Procedures

High-grade composite fillings, root canal therapy, crowns, and structural tooth restorations designed for durability.

Cosmetic Smile Enhancements

Professional teeth whitening, aesthetic alignment advice, and custom cosmetic solutions for natural-looking smiles.

Periodontal & Gum Care

Targeted treatment for gingivitis, deep scaling, root planing, and preventative maintenance for healthy gums.

Charitable Community Outreach

Leading diagnostic camps and accessible dental health programs hosted by Mother Teresa Memorial Dental Hospital.

Oral Health Counseling

Personalized dietary and home-care guidance to help patients maintain optimal oral hygiene habits lifelong.
