About Radadiya Nishit

Crafting Bespoke Environments That Inspire & Endure

As a Senior Interior Designer at Octium design studio, Radadiya Nishit orchestrates comprehensive spatial transformations. With an uncompromising eye for proportion, material harmony, and lighting architecture, Radadiya redefines luxury residential and modern commercial environments.

Every design concept is tailored around individual lifestyle ergonomics and brand identities, ensuring every detail—from custom FF&E curation to structural spatial flow—exudes sophistication and purpose.
