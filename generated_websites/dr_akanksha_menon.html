About Dr. Akanksha Menon

Advancing Oral Health Science Through Non-Clinical Innovation

Dr. Akanksha Menon specializes in non-clinical dental methodologies and Ayush fellowship initiatives. With a strong commitment to public health strategy, interdisciplinary medical research, and integrative healthcare systems, Dr. Menon plays a critical role in bridging traditional healing frameworks with modern diagnostic and therapeutic protocols.

Her fellowship focus explores the broader impact of holistic care paradigms, quality management in healthcare, and operational excellence in non-clinical settings.
