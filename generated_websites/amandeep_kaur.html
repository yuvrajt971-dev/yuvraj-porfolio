About Dr. Amandeep Kaur

Dedicated to Personalized Dental Excellence & Patient Comfort

As an Associate Dentist at Dr. Navneet's multispeciality dental clinic, Dr. Amandeep Kaur combines clinical rigor with a compassionate approach. She believes every smile is unique and deserves bespoke care customized to individual patient needs.

Specializing in modern restorative techniques, endodontics, and aesthetic smile enhancements, Dr. Kaur ensures every visit is relaxing, transparent, and built around long-term oral health.
