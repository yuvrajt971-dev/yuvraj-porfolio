About Dr. Megan Lourdes

Passionate Healthcare Professional Committed to Clinical Precision

Currently serving as a Compulsory Rotatory Residential Intern (CRRI) at Sathyabama University Dental College And Hospital, Dr. Megan Lourdes blends academic rigor with compassionate, patient-centered clinical practice.

With hands-on experience across multiple specialized departments including Endodontics, Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery, Prosthodontics, and Periodontics, Dr. Megan focuses on delivering evidence-based treatments utilizing modern dental technologies.
