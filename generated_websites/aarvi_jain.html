About Aarvi Jain

Crafting Sophisticated Interiors That Tell a Distinct Story.

As an Interior Designer at Design Converse, Aarvi Jain specializes in bridging conceptual brilliance with architectural precision. Her portfolio spans curated residential sanctuaries, modern executive suites, and luxury experiential spaces.

With an intuitive eye for scale, light interplay, tactile natural textures, and bespoke lighting setups, Aarvi transforms blank layouts into immersive environments tailored to every client's unique lifestyle.

Interior Design Services

Elevating spatial experiences through specialized design solutions tailored for discerning clients and corporate spaces.

Residential Spatial Design

End-to-end luxury home interior concepts, from space planning and palette selection to fine joinery details and furniture curation.

Commercial Interiors

High-performance office, lounge, and showroom environments built around brand identity, productivity, and modern ergonomics.

Material & Texture Styling
