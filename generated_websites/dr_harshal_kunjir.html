Surgical Services

Core Clinical Specializations

Comprehensive surgical care tailored to facial structure, function, and cosmetic perfection.

Orthognathic Surgery

Surgical correction of jaw irregularities to improve facial aesthetics, bite alignment, and breathing functions.

  • Malocclusion Correction
  • Asymmetry Alignment
  • Sleep Apnea Relief

Facial Trauma & Injury

Emergency and elective management of facial bone fractures, lacerations, and post-traumatic soft tissue defects.

  • Jaw & Cheekbone Fractures
  • Soft Tissue Repair
  • Scar Revision

Dental Implants

  • Full-Arch Restoration
  • Bone Augmentation
  • Sinus Lift Procedures

  • Joint Reconstruction
  • Occlusal Therapy
