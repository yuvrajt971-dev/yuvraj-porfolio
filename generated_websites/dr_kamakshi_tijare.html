About Dr. Kamakshi Tijare

Dedicated Dental Professional Focused on Patient Health & Clinical Excellence

Dr. Kamakshi Tijare is a distinguished Junior Resident currently serving at the prestigious Index Institute of Dental Sciences in Indore. With a profound passion for modern dentistry, she combines empathetic patient communication with rigorous clinical methodology.

Her clinical residence focuses on comprehensive diagnostic workups, preventive care, restorative treatments, and state-of-the-art procedures designed to enhance oral health and restore patient confidence.
