Dr. Aishwarya Sharma is a renowned Consulting Prosthodontist specializing in complex full-mouth rehabilitations, dental implants, maxillofacial prosthetics, and aesthetic smile transformations.

Specialized Solutions

Comprehensive Prosthodontic Services

Tailored clinical treatments designed to restore smile function and enhance confidence.

Dental Implants & Restorations

Single and multiple tooth replacement using state-of-the-art implant solutions, ensuring long-lasting stability and natural feeling.

Full Mouth Rehabilitation

Comprehensive reconstruction for severely worn, damaged, or missing teeth, re-establishing occlusion and natural jaw alignment.

Esthetic Smile Design

Custom porcelain veneers, bonding, and aesthetic contouring for a vibrant, harmonious, and personalized smile design.

Crowns & Fixed Bridges

High-precision zircornia and ceramic crowns designed to seamlessly match natural tooth shade, strength, and translucency.

TMJ & Occlusal Therapy

Diagnosis and non-invasive treatment of temporomandibular joint disorders, bite discrepancies, and bruxism.

Maxillofacial Prosthetics

Specialized prosthetic rehabilitation for congenital defects, trauma, or post-surgical oral/facial reconstruction.
