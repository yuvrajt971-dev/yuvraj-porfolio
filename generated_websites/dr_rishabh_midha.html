About Dr. Rishabh Midha

Crafting Healthy, Confident Smiles with Advanced Science

Dr. Rishabh Midha is a renowned Orthodontist affiliated with Panjab University, Chandigarh. Combining advanced digital diagnostic technology with customized aligner treatments, he specializes in diagnosing, preventing, and treating dental and facial irregularities.

Whether working with young adults or professionals seeking discrete invisible aligners, Dr. Midha delivers holistic clinical solutions tailored to each unique profile.

Clinical Treatments

Specialized Orthodontic Care

Comprehensive solutions designed to bring harmony to your facial aesthetics and dental function.

Clear Invisible Aligners

Custom 3D-designed clear trays that gently shift teeth into optimal alignment discreetly and comfortably without visible brackets.

Fixed Braces System

High-precision ceramic and self-ligating metal braces for efficient correction of complex crowding and bite alignment.

Dentofacial Orthopedics

Growth modification treatments during youth to guide jaw development and balance facial structure naturally.

Digital Smile Design

Aesthetic evaluation and virtual treatment simulation allowing patients to preview their perfect smile before starting.

Interceptive Orthodontics

Early childhood evaluation to correct jaw growth issues, habits, and prevent more complex treatment in adulthood.

TMJ & Retention Therapy

Targeted therapy for jaw joint dysfunction, bite stabilization, and post-orthodontic custom retainers for long-term stability.
