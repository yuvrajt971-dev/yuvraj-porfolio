Professional Background

Bridging Academic Rigor with Patient-Centered Care

As a Senior Lecturer at Goa Dental College & Hospital Bambolim, Dr. Atrey Pai Khot combines clinical acumen with a strong passion for medical education. Dr. Khot is committed to advancing oral health outcomes through comprehensive instruction, hands-on clinical training, and evidence-based practice.

With an emphasis on modern dental techniques and patient comfort, Dr. Khot plays an instrumental role in shaping competent, compassionate healthcare professionals while contributing to institutional research and public health initiatives.
