BACKGROUND & PHILOSOPHY

Dedicated to Saving Natural Teeth with Advanced Dental Science

Dr. Aditya Narayanan serves as a Consultant Endodontist at PES University Hospital. His clinical practice focuses on root canal therapy, complex endodontic retreatments, management of dental trauma, and surgical endodontics.

With an emphasis on patient comfort, biomechanical accuracy, and micro-dentistry, Dr. Narayanan employs modern magnification tools and rotary instrumentation to deliver painless, precise, and long-lasting outcomes for every patient.

Specialized Endodontic Procedures

Comprehensive care focused on preserving your natural teeth using advanced techniques.

Painless Root Canal Therapy

Single-visit and multi-visit root canal treatments utilizing rotary nickel-titanium files and 3D obturation for total comfort.

Micro-Endodontic Surgery
