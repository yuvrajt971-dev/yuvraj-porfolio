Dedicated to Gentle, High-Quality Oral Healthcare

As a General Dentist at Naushad khatri medical centre, Dr. Gayathri combines clinical skill with a compassionate approach to ensure every patient receives top-tier dental treatment in a comfortable environment.

Clinical Capabilities

General & Preventive Care

Routine dental examinations, deep scaling, polishing, and early diagnosis to maintain optimal oral health.
