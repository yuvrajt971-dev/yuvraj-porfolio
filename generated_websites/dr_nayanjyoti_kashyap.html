About Dr. Kashyap

Mastering Precision Dental Surgery with Compassionate Care

Dr. Nayanjyoti Kashyap is a highly accomplished Master of Dental Surgery (MDS) professional affiliated with the renowned **ITS Centre for Dental Studies and Research**. Combining state-of-the-art clinical methodologies with an unwavering commitment to patient safety, Dr. Kashyap delivers top-tier diagnostic, restorative, and surgical oral healthcare.

With an emphasis on minimally invasive techniques, complex dental rehabilitations, and aesthetic perfection, Dr. Kashyap remains at the forefront of contemporary dental advancements through continuous research and clinical practice.

Specialized Offerings

Comprehensive Dental & Surgical Services

Delivering world-class dental interventions tailored to restore functionality, aesthetics, and long-term oral health.

Maxillofacial & Oral Surgery

Expert surgical management of wisdom tooth extractions, jaw alignment issues, facial trauma, and complex surgical interventions.

Dental Implantology

Precision placement of biocompatible titanium implants for durable, natural-looking tooth replacement and full arch restoration.

Cosmetic Smile Design

Transformative aesthetic procedures including porcelain veneers, tooth whitening, laser gum contouring, and smile makeovers.

Precision Endodontics

Microscopic root canal therapies designed to preserve natural tooth structure with absolute comfort and speed.

Prosthodontic Care

Custom crowns, bridges, and complete denture prosthetics engineered for perfect bite alignment and seamless functional harmony.

Periodontal Laser Therapy

Advanced gum disease treatments, pocket reduction, and regenerative soft-tissue therapies utilizing precision laser technology.
