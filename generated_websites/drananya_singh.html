About Dr. Ananya Singh

Blending Artistry with Advanced Oral Surgery

Dr. Ananya Singh is the Co-founder and Lead Dental Surgeon at Madhavas Dental Care. Dedicated to providing compassionate, highly advanced clinical care, she specializes in aesthetic smile design, complex surgical interventions, and digital implantology.

Under her leadership, Madhavas Dental Care has evolved into a premier destination for personalized dental treatments. Dr. Singh incorporates state-of-the-art diagnostic imaging and painless laser surgical techniques to ensure maximum comfort and long-lasting oral health.
