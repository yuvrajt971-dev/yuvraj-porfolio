About Dr. Sidharth Manoj

Pioneering Advanced Oral & Jaw Reconstruction

As an acclaimed Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeon at Anjali Dental Clinic and Implant Centre, Dr. Sidharth Manoj integrates surgical precision with modern digital dentistry techniques.

With extensive training in complex facial trauma, jaw corrective surgery, wisdom tooth impactions, and immediate dental implants, Dr. Sidharth delivers comprehensive clinical care designed around patient comfort and predictable visual aesthetics.

Computer-assisted planning for absolute precision in implants.

Pain-Free Protocols

Specialized Surgical Services

Delivering comprehensive treatments utilizing world-class technology and patient-first protocols.

Dental Implantology

Single, multi-unit, and full-mouth All-on-4 implants using premium biocompatible titanium and zirconia materials.

  • Immediate Loading Implants
  • Bone Grafting & Sinus Lifts

Orthognathic Surgery

Corrective jaw surgery to restore proper function, address sleep apnea, and enhance facial structural balance.

  • Jaw Alignment Correction
  • Facial Asymmetry Treatment

Wisdom Tooth Impactions

Safe and painless surgical extraction of deeply impacted third molars with minimal post-operative recovery time.

  • Pain-Free Nerve Preservation
  • Rapid Recovery Protocols

Facial Trauma & Fracture Care

  • Emergency Trauma Surgery
  • Aesthetic Soft Tissue Repair
