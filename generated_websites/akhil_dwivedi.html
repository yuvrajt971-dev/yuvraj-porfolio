About Dr. Akhil Dwivedi

Delivering Pain-Free, Precision Dentistry with Integrity

Dr. Akhil Dwivedi is a distinguished Senior Dental Surgeon currently leading dental surgical procedures at Agrawal Nursing Home. With years of hands-on clinical mastery, Dr. Dwivedi specializes in restorative dentistry, complex surgical extractions, modern root canal therapy, and aesthetic smile enhancements.

By utilizing advanced dental technology and maintaining strict clinical protocols, Dr. Dwivedi ensures every patient experiences minimal discomfort, pristine outcomes, and personalized treatment plans tailored to individual needs.
