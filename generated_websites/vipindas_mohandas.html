About My Practice

Transforming Digital Scans into Anatomical Masterpieces

In modern restorative dentistry, accuracy isn't just a requirement—it’s the cornerstone of patient confidence. At Digitace Dental Pvt. Ltd, I utilize state-of-the-art CAD/CAM technologies to model, design, and produce high-tier dental prosthetics that seamlessly blend aesthetics with functional precision.

My expertise spans full-contour zirconia crowns, custom implant abutments, aesthetic porcelain veneers, and complex bridge frameworks. Every restoration undergoes rigid quality checks to ensure exact marginal fit, occlusion alignment, and life-like shade shading.

Technical Capabilities & Services

Delivering comprehensive digital laboratory services tailored to clinical perfection and patient satisfaction.

CAD/CAM Restoration Design

Expert 3D modeling using industry-leading dental software to render anatomical crowns, frameworks, and inlay/onlays with micronic precision.

Zirconia & Ceramic Crowns

Fabrication of high-translucency full-contour zirconia, E-max, and layered ceramic restorations tailored for strength and natural aesthetics.

Implant Prosthetics

Margin & Fit Optimization
