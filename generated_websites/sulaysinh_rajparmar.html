About Sulaysinh

Driving Strategic Governance & Student Leadership at NOSPlan

As a dedicated **Convention Officer** for **NOSPlan (Organisation of Students of Planning)**, Sulaysinh Rajparmar plays a pivotal role in organizing high-impact national gatherings that connect urban planning students, academicians, and industry experts.

With an unwavering focus on spatial innovation, collaborative governance, and policy dialogues, Sulaysinh ensures that each convention serves as a catalyst for creative urban solutions and career leadership for aspiring planners across the country.
