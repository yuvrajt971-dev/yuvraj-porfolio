About Dr. Kaviya Natarajan

Delivering Exceptional Oral Healthcare at Dr.Sunil Dental Health Centre

As an Associate Dentist at Dr.Sunil (Dr.Raman Dental Health Centre), Dr. Kaviya Natarajan is committed to offering patient-centered care. She specializes in preventive care, restorative procedures, and aesthetic smile enhancements, ensuring every patient enjoys optimal oral health and confidence.

Preventive Dentistry

Routine cleanings, early detection, and comprehensive oral examinations.

Cosmetic Care

Teeth whitening, aesthetic restorations, and custom smile redesigns.

Clinical Specialties

Comprehensive Dental Services

Tailored, high-precision treatments using modern techniques for all your oral care needs.

General & Preventive Care

Thorough dental check-ups, deep scalings, cavity prevention, and custom fluoride treatments to maintain optimal oral wellness.

Cosmetic Smile Enhancement

Brighten and refine your smile with professional whitening, composite bonding, and aesthetic restorations.

Restorative Dentistry

Repair damaged or decayed teeth with tooth-colored fillings, durable crowns, and protective restorative procedures.

Gum Health & Hygiene

Focused periodontal therapy to treat early-stage gum diseases, preventing tooth loss and promoting gum health.

Pediatric Dental Care

Gentle and reassuring dental treatments for children, fostering early habits for a lifetime of healthy smiles.

Emergency Care

Prompt clinical intervention for acute toothaches, chipped teeth, and other urgent dental issues.
