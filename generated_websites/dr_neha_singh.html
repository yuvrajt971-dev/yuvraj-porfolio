About Dr. Neha Singh

Bridging Academic Excellence with Next-Gen Startup Incubation

As the Student Coordinator at IIHMR Startups, Dr. Neha Singh plays a pivotal role in shaping student innovation, guiding early-stage entrepreneurs, and establishing structured pathways for healthtech startups to grow from ideation to scalable enterprises.

With deep expertise in ecosystem management, student leadership development, and strategic networking, Dr. Singh facilitates crucial linkages between academic talent, industry mentors, and venture capital resources.
