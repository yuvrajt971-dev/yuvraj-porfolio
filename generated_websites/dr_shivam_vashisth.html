About Dr. Shivam Vashisth

Bridging Cutting-Edge Dental Innovation with Hands-On Clinical Practice

At Nobel Biocare, Dr. Shivam Vashisth spearheads high-impact training and educational initiatives designed for dental professionals, implantologists, and surgical specialists.

With a deep commitment to continuous medical education, Dr. Vashisth focuses on translating sophisticated surgical methodologies, digital workflows, and CAD/CAM prosthetics into practical, high-precision learning modules for practitioners worldwide.
