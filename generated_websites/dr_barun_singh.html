Dedicated to Transforming Patient Care Benchmarks

As a Quality and Patient Safety Intern at the Delhi State Health Mission, Dr. Barun Singh plays a pivotal role in reviewing clinical protocols, analyzing safety metrics, and championing continuous quality improvement initiatives across health facilities.

By combining medical knowledge with administrative quality frameworks (NQAS, NABH), Dr. Singh ensures that public health infrastructure adheres to rigorous safety parameters, minimizing healthcare-associated risks and maximizing patient satisfaction.
