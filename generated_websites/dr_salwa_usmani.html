About Dr. Salwa Usmani

Commitment to Patient Healthcare & Clinical Mastery

As a motivated and meticulous Dental Intern at the renowned Buddha Institute of Dental Sciences & Hospital, Dr. Salwa Usmani combines academic rigor with hands-on surgical and restorative expertise.

Driven by a desire to bring pain-free treatments and aesthetic perfection to patients, her clinical rotations encompass comprehensive oral diagnosis, endodontics, prosthodontics, periodontics, and pediatric dental management.
