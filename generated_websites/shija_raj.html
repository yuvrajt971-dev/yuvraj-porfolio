About Shija Raj

Elevating Dental Research Standards Through Dedicated Scientific Officer Leadership

As the **Local Scientific Officer at the Indian Association of Dental Students (IADS)**, Shija Raj leads critical initiatives aimed at fostering scientific research, peer review, and academic exchange among aspiring dental surgeons and researchers across the region.

With a strong commitment to evidence-based practice and academic inquiry, Shija spearheads paper presentations, scientific workshops, and clinical symposia that empower dental students to present groundbreaking research on global stages.

Inter-College Collaborations
