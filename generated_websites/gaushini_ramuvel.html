About & Mission

Bridging Scientific Rigor and Healthcare Value Assessment

As a Peer Reviewer at ISPOR, Gaushini Ramuvel plays a pivotal role in critically appraising cutting-edge scientific manuscripts, pharmacoeconomic evaluations, and health policy submissions.

With a deep commitment to academic precision, Gaushini evaluates methodologies including decision-tree analyses, Markov modeling, real-world evidence (RWE) studies, and comparative effectiveness research—ensuring that published literature drives meaningful clinical and economic impact.

Critical Scientific Appraisal

Evaluating statistical robustness, epidemiological parameters, and study designs.

Health Economics & Value Frameworks

Validating cost-effectiveness, budget impact models, and decision-analytic methods.

Key Capabilities

Areas of Expertise & Editorial Services

Providing expert peer review, academic evaluation, and HEOR consultation grounded in rigorous scientific methodology.

Peer Review Excellence

Objective, thorough, and constructive review of manuscripts submitted to top-tier healthcare journals and conferences.

Economic Modeling Review

In-depth critique of cost-effectiveness models (CEA), cost-utility models (CUA), and budget impact assessments (BIA).

Real-World Evidence (RWE)

Assessment of observational studies, electronic health record (EHR) analyses, and patient registry evaluations.

PRO & Quality of Life Analysis

Evaluation of Patient-Reported Outcomes (PROs), utility scoring methods, and quality-adjusted life year (QALY) measurements.

Health Policy & HTA

Analyzing Health Technology Assessment (HTA) frameworks, reimbursement strategies, and global policy implications.

Methodological Guidance

Providing guidance to ensure research aligns with international reporting standards such as CHEERS and STROBE.
