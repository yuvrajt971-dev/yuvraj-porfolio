About Dr. Alan

Transforming Oral Health Through Academic Precision & Practical Expertise

Dr. Alan Eldho Paul is currently undergoing clinical internship at the prestigious Amrita School of Dentistry. With a deep passion for diagnostic accuracy, preventive oral health, and modern restorative procedures, Dr. Alan combines rigorous clinical discipline with a reassuring patient approach.

His internship spans multi-specialty rotations including Periodontics, Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery, Prosthodontics, and Pediatric Dentistry, staying on the cutting edge of modern dental technologies and surgical protocols.
