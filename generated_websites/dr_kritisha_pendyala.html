About Dr. Kritisha Pendyala

Dedicated to Gentle, High-Quality Family & General Dental Care

As an independent General Dentist, Dr. Kritisha Pendyala is committed to elevating patient care through preventative treatments, restorative procedures, and personalized dental education.

Every smile is unique. Dr. Pendyala focuses on creating a relaxing, anxiety-free clinical environment where patients receive clear guidance and tailored care plans designed to maintain optimal oral wellness across all stages of life.

Clinical Specialties

Comprehensive Dental Services

From routine preventive maintenance to advanced restorative care, explore the full spectrum of dental procedures offered.

Preventive Oral Care

Professional routine scaling, polishing, dental examinations, and fluoride treatments to preserve your natural smile.

  • Routine Checkups & Cleaning
  • Dental X-Rays & Screenings

Cosmetic Enhancement

Transformative teeth whitening, aesthetic bonding, and veneers to boost your confidence with a brighter smile.

  • In-Clinic Teeth Whitening
  • Aesthetic Composite Bonding

Restorative Dentistry

Tooth-colored fillings, durable crowns, and bridges tailored to restore strength and function to damaged teeth.

  • Dental Composite Fillings
  • Porcelain Crowns & Bridges

Endodontic Care

Targeted root canal treatments designed to relieve acute dental pain and preserve infected natural teeth safely.

  • Pain-Free Root Canal Therapy
  • Pulp Treatment & Infection Control

Periodontal & Gum Care

Diagnosis and non-surgical therapy for gum inflammation, gingivitis, and periodontitis to ensure foundation health.

  • Deep Scaling & Root Planing
  • Gum Health Maintenance Plans

Emergency Dental Care

Prompt clinical attention for sudden toothaches, chipped teeth, lost fillings, and urgent oral discomfort.

  • Urgent Pain Management
  • Emergency Extractions & Repairs
