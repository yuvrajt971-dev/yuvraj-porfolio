ABOUT THE SPECIALIST

Bridging Clinical Expertise & Pathological Precision

Dr. Akshay Trimukhe is a highly accomplished Senior Resident in Oral and Maxillofacial Pathology and Oral Microbiology at the prestigious All India Institute of Medical Sciences (AIIMS). With an unwavering commitment to diagnostic perfection, Dr. Trimukhe specializes in identifying complex head and neck lesions, pre-cancerous tissue alterations, and rare microbial mucosal conditions.

Combining academic rigor with clinical acumen, his work aids surgeons, oncologists, and clinicians in making critical treatment decisions through definitive tissue diagnosis and histopathological reporting.

Histopathological Diagnosis

Expert tissue section analysis for benign & malignant lesions.

Oral Microbiome & Cultures

Identification of fungal, viral, and bacterial mucosal infections.

CLINICAL SERVICES & RESEARCH

Specialized Clinical Services

Delivering diagnostic clarity and research insights across oral medicine and pathology.

Oral Oncopathology

Early detection and microscopic differentiation of oral squamous cell carcinoma, dysplasias, and pre-malignant lesions.

Biopsy Diagnostics

Comprehensive histological analysis of incisional, excisional, and punch biopsies with rapid turn-around reporting.

Oral Microbiology

Diagnostic workup for oral candidiasis, actinomycosis, deep fungal pathogens, and rare bacterial manifestations.

Odontogenic Tumors & Cysts

Precise classification and diagnosis of jaw bone pathologies, ameloblastomas, odontomas, and developmental cysts.

Academic Research

Contributes to peer-reviewed publications, clinical case series, and innovative pathology research at AIIMS.

Second Opinion Review

Expert second-opinion consultations on complex slides, ambiguous tissue staining, and difficult diagnoses.
