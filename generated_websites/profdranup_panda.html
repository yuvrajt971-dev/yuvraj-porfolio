As Clinical Director at The Tooth Fairy, Prof. Dr. Anup Panda combines years of academic rigor with state-of-the-art clinical practice. Specializing in advanced implantology, cosmetic reconstructions, and comprehensive oral surgery, Dr. Panda is committed to providing painless, precise, and patient-centered care.

Beyond the chairside, Dr. Panda contributes actively to dental research and academic mentorship, training the next generation of dental surgeons with a philosophy rooted in ethics, modern technology, and compassionate treatment.
