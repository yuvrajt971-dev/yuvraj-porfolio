About Dr. Khan

Dedicated Dental Professional Leading Outreach Initiatives

As a General Dentist leading camp initiatives at Smile Foundation, Dr. Atiya Khan brings compassion, clinical accuracy, and preventive care strategies to populations in need. She specializes in conducting comprehensive dental check-ups, administering essential treatments, and organizing community awareness camps.

Core Clinical Capabilities

Comprehensive Dental Services

Combining clinical accuracy with gentle care in every environment.

Community Dental Camps

Organizing and executing large-scale mobile dental camps equipped for screenings, treatments, and referrals in outreach programs.

General & Preventive Dentistry

Comprehensive examinations, routine cleanings, fluoride treatments, and scaling to preserve tooth health and prevent decay.

Oral Diagnostics & Screening

Precise diagnosis of dental caries, periodontal diseases, and initial screening for oral mucosal lesions.

Restorative Procedures

Composite and amalgam cavity fillings, temporary restorations, and immediate relief treatments for dental pain.

Oral Health Workshops

Educational sessions for children and adults focusing on proper brushing techniques, diet habits, and preventive oral care.

Emergency Care Relief

On-site management of acute toothaches, dental trauma stabilization, and referral management to specialized centers.
