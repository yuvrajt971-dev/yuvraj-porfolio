About Dr. Harshitha

Combining Medical Expertise with Strategic Venture Growth

As Program Manager for Healthtech at **Anthill Ventures**, Dr. Harshitha Balasubramanian leads initiatives that source, accelerate, and scale disruptive healthtech startups. Combining a deep understanding of healthcare dynamics with venture building strategies, she helps founders navigate regulatory pathways, market entry, and commercialization.

Anthill Ventures scales early-stage startups by bridging speed and scale. Dr. Harshitha acts as a catalyst in this ecosystem, facilitating strategic partnerships, investor relations, and clinical validation programs.
