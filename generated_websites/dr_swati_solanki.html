About Dr. Swati Solanki

Merging Clinical Expertise with Pain-Free Dental Conservation

Dr. Swati Solanki is an accomplished Postgraduate Resident specializing in Conservative Dentistry and Endodontics at Inderprastha Dental College & Hospital. Her clinical philosophy revolves around maximal preservation of natural tooth structures, adopting cutting-edge endodontic techniques, and mitigating patient anxiety through evidence-based treatments.

With extensive training in rotary endodontics, biomimetic restorations, micro-surgical endodontics, and aesthetic smile enhancements, Dr. Solanki combines technical excellence with personalized patient care.
