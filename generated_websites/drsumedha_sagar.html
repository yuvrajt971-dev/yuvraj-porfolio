ABOUT DR. SUMEDHA SAGAR

Transforming Smiles Through Modern Science & Artistry

Dr. Sumedha Sagar is a renowned Cosmetic Dentist practicing at Ryan Clinic : Skin and Hair Transplant Clinic. With a profound passion for aesthetic facial harmony, Dr. Sagar combines cutting-edge dental technology with a artistic perspective to craft natural, radiant, and enduring smiles.

Whether specializing in porcelain veneers, digital smile design, teeth whitening, or complete oral aesthetic rehabilitations, every treatment is tailored to accentuate each patient's unique facial features and boost self-confidence.

SPECIALIZED CLINICAL SERVICES

Custom Tailored Cosmetic Treatments

Delivering precision dental procedures using world-class protocols and advanced materials.

Digital Smile Makeovers

Comprehensive smile evaluation and 3D digital planning to construct perfectly symmetrical, natural-looking tooth proportion and shade.

Porcelain Veneers & Crowns

Minimal-prep, custom-crafted ceramic veneers that fix discoloration, gaps, and chips for a flawless red-carpet smile.

Laser Teeth Whitening
