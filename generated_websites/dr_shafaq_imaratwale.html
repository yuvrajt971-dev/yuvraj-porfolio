About Dr. Shafaq

Transforming Smiles Through Clinical Excellence & Patient Empathy

As an Associate Dentist at HaiDent, Dr. Shafaq Imaratwale combines advanced clinical methodology with a gentle, patient-centric approach. Believing that dental health is integral to overall well-being, Dr. Shafaq specializes in comprehensive oral evaluations, preventive care, and aesthetic procedures tailored to every individual.

Patient-First Philosophy: Ensuring stress-free, painless treatments for patients of all ages.
