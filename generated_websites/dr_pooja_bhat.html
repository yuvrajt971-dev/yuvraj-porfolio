About Dr. Pooja Bhat

Excellence in Modern Clinical Dentistry & Oral Health

As an Associate Dentist at Reformface Dental Clinic, Dr. Pooja Bhat delivers exceptional comprehensive dental care tailored to each patient's unique needs. With a strong commitment to clinical precision, comfort, and aesthetics, Dr. Bhat transforms smiles while prioritizing long-term oral health.

From gentle preventive treatments to advanced cosmetic procedures, her approach blends cutting-edge techniques with a welcoming, stress-free clinical environment.
