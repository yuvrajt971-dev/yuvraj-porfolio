About Dr. Madhuri

Dedicated to Excellence in Oral Healthcare & Surgery

As a Dental Intern at the prestigious CSMSS Dental College And Hospital, Dr. Madhuri Munde brings together a high standard of clinical care, meticulous surgical precision, and a gentle empathetic touch for every patient.

Trained in comprehensive diagnostic procedures, preventive dentistry, operative root canal treatments, and oral surgery support, Dr. Madhuri is committed to advancing patient wellness and restoring confident smiles.

Capabilities

Clinical Services & Expertise

Comprehensive dental procedures delivered with modern tools and compassionate clinical skills.

General & Preventive Dentistry

Routine dental checkups, scaling, prophylaxis, oral health screenings, and fluoridation treatment for long-term health.

Restorative & Operative Care

Composite fillings, tooth restorations, cavity preparations, and aesthetic tooth preservation techniques.

Endodontic Support

Assisting in root canal therapy, biomechanical preparation, and post-endodontic restorative rehabilitation.

Oral Surgery & Extractions

Minor oral surgical procedures, painless dental extractions, suture management, and post-operative care.

Pediatric Dentistry

Gentle dental management for children, preventive sealants, habits counseling, and anxiety-free treatment.

Patient Hygiene Education

Educating patients on oral hygiene practices, dietary guidelines, and disease prevention strategies.
