About Dr. Mohab

Dedicated to Elevating Public Oral Health Standards

As a Dentist serving under the Ministry of Health & Population in Egypt, Dr. Mohab Adel combines clinical precision with compassionate patient care. He works diligently to make high-quality oral healthcare accessible, pain-free, and personalized for every individual.

From diagnostic evaluation to advanced restorative care, Dr. Mohab emphasizes prevention, education, and state-of-the-art procedures to ensure long-lasting patient satisfaction.

Specialized Services

Clinical Expertise & Treatment

Comprehensive healthcare solutions backed by clinical rigour and modern dental practices.

Preventative Dentistry

Routine check-ups, deep cleanings, fluoride treatments, and oral hygiene education to stop dental issues before they start.

Restorative Dentistry

Composite fillings, crowns, and bridges designed to restore complete tooth functionality while maintaining natural aesthetics.

Cosmetic Enhancement

Professional teeth whitening, aesthetic bonding, and smile transformations tailored to boost patient confidence.

Endodontic Care

Advanced root canal therapy designed to alleviate pain, save natural teeth, and resolve internal infections safely.

Public Health Initiatives

Community healthcare outreach programs with the Ministry of Health & Population focused on oral health literacy.

Pediatric Dental Care

Gentle, patient-centered dental care for children and young adults to build early positive oral care habits.
