About Dr. Gaurav Kumar Jha

Bridging Medical Science, Research, and Public Health Outcomes

As a Senior Research Officer at the prestigious Postgraduate Institute of Medical Education and Research (PGIMER), Chandigarh, Dr. Gaurav Kumar Jha leads transformative clinical and epidemiological research initiatives.

With an unwavering commitment to scientific rigor, his work encompasses multi-center clinical trials, biometric health statistics, systematic evidence synthesis, and translational healthcare policy formulations designed to elevate patient outcomes across the region.
