Profile Summary

Advancing Oncological Practice through Precision, Innovation, and Compassion

As a Senior Resident in the Department of Head and Neck Surgical Oncology at the Cancer Hospital & Research Institute, Dr. Tamoghna Jana is dedicated to high-precision surgical intervention and progressive patient management.

With an unwavering commitment to clinical efficacy and surgical accuracy, Dr. Jana actively participates in complex resections, reconstructive oncological procedures, and multi-specialty tumor boards to formulate individualized care plans.

Specialized Capabilities

Clinical Focus & Surgical Specializations

Delivering patient-centric care backed by modern surgical techniques and evidence-based medicine.

Head & Neck Resection

Expertise in complex tumor extirpation, neck dissections, and specialized surgical procedures for oral, laryngeal, and thyroid malignancies.

Reconstructive Procedures

Surgical planning and execution focused on post-resection functional restoration and aesthetic outcomes using modern flap techniques.

Multidisciplinary Tumor Board

Post-Operative & Critical Care
