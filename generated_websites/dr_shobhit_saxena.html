About DR. SHOBHIT SAXENA

Pioneering Modern Orthodontics & Patient Care

As a specialized Orthodontist at Sankalchand Patel University, Dr. Shobhit Saxena integrates clinical mastery with state-of-the-art technological innovations. His practice is centered around precision alignment, functional harmony, and long-term aesthetic beauty.

Combining academic rigor with personalized patient treatments, Dr. Saxena ensures each patient receives comprehensive orthodontic care—ranging from invisible aligners to complex dentofacial orthopedics.
